The Approach

We don't fight the tide. We catalyze its force into a wave you can ride.

A disciplined, repeatable method for turning public policy catalysts into concentrated, well-understood positions—and turbulence into structured opportunity.

The Thesis

Markets, like the ocean, move in powerful, unpredictable cycles.

Trying to fight the momentum of global economic shifts is inefficient; ignoring it is dangerous. At Tidebreak Capital, our strategy is built on resilience, balance, and tactical absorption.

In Life Sciences, some of the most powerful catalysts aren't discovered in the lab—they're set in Washington. Coverage decisions, guidance revisions, approvals, and reimbursement policy re-rate entire categories. The generalist market treats these as tail risk; the clinical specialist under-weights them. We read both as one connected system, and price what that system moves.

The Framework

Four pillars, run as one system.

01

A repeatable catalyst engine

Policy-driven catalysts are run as a defined process rather than one-off bets. We maintain a live map of the events Washington puts on the calendar, a consistent method for pricing each one, and disciplined sizing rules—so the edge is a system, not a hunch.

02

Public information only — compliance as moat

We profit from being early on public policy, never from non-public government information. Everything we act on is on the public record. The discipline isn't a constraint on the edge—it is the edge, and the barrier that keeps most of the market out.

03

Interpretive vantage you can't buy on a terminal

Data feeds are commoditized; judgment is not. Our read on how Washington actually moves is built from the seam between the science and the politics—an interpretive vantage that compounds with every cycle and can't be licensed by a competitor.

04

Structure follows strategy

Policy catalysts are lumpy and non-linear. Our entity and liquidity design is built around that shape—patient capital matched to the opportunity, so we're never a forced seller into the very turbulence we're built to absorb.

How A Position Gets Made

The work happens before capital is committed.

01 · Map

Build the catalyst calendar

We translate the regulatory and legislative calendar into a live map of dated, public policy events—and the businesses each one could re-rate.

02 · Diligence

Clinical & market diligence

We pressure-test the science and the business model together: how the product works, how the company actually earns its returns, and how policy changes the math.

03 · Price

Price the catalyst

We estimate what the market has—and hasn't—discounted, and define what would have to be true for the thesis to break open.

04 · Size

Concentrate with discipline

Conviction earns concentration. Positions are sized to the strength of the read and the risk to principal, never to excitement.

05 · Hold

Deliberately unhurried

We give the catalyst room to play out, absorbing turbulence rather than trading it—capturing the growth while others are shaken out.
